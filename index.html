~600–700 kcal · 50–60 g protein
Turkey panini

2 slices whole-grain bread + 5–6 oz turkey + cheese, spinach, mustard and apple.

~500–600 kcal · 40–50 g protein

Food safety: refrigerate cooked food promptly, use most refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days, and freeze later portions.

What success looks like

By January: establish consistency and move toward ~170 lb if appropriate. By March: aim for a leaner waist and preserved strength, whether the scale says 165 or 170. April: maintain, recover, and enjoy your wedding.

If weight and waist are unchanged for 2–3 consistent weeks, slightly reduce carb/fat portions or add walking. If strength and recovery suffer, increase food.
